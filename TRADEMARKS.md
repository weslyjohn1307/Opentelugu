1. **Necessity:** The legacy character formats and keyboard layouts cannot be readily identified by end users without referencing these widely recognized industry standard names.
2. **Proportionality:** Only the minimum textual reference necessary to identify technical compatibility is used. No third-party logos, wordmarks, trade dress, or stylized branding are used.
3. **Absence of Endorsement:** No association, sponsorship, certification, or endorsement by the trademark holders is implied, claimed, or exists.
